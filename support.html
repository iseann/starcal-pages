Calendar Access

StarCal requests full calendar access so it can show your Apple Calendar events and save new events you create in the app.

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  • Find StarCal.
  • Enable full access to Calendar if you want StarCal to display and create events.

Common Questions

Does StarCal require an account?

No. StarCal does not require you to create an account.

Where are my events stored?

Events are read from and saved to Apple Calendar on your device and iCloud calendar account, depending on your Apple Calendar settings.

Why are some calendars unavailable?

Some subscribed or read-only calendars do not allow apps to add or edit events. Choose a writable calendar when creating a new event.
